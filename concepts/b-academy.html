    Who's who

    Mentors commit to

      Recording policy

      1. Mentors record VOD review sessions so the scene can learn from them.
      2. Coaches ask every player before recording their voice.
      3. If a player says no, they're muted on Discord, and the coach restates each question they ask so the recording keeps its context.
      4. Every review gets a short TL;DR, written during or after the session.

      No surprise recordings. Ever.
